# Warranty policy

## What is covered
Gear sold by Pinecrest Supply (jackets, boots, tents, headlamps, trekking poles and bottles) has a one-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, starting on the delivery date.

## What is not covered
The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, accidents, misuse, alterations, or damage from improper care. Apparel basics such as hoodies, beanies and socks are not covered by the warranty.

## How to make a warranty claim
Email warranty@pinecrest.example with your order number, a short description of the problem, and photos of the defect. Our warranty team replies within 3 business days and will repair, replace or refund the item at its discretion.

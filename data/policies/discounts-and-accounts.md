# Discount codes and account help

## Using discount codes
Only one discount code can be used per order. A code can be added to an order after checkout only while the order has not shipped yet. Once an order ships, codes cannot be added.

## Expired and future codes
Expired codes cannot be honored, and codes cannot be used before their start date. We do not create new codes or custom discounts through support.

## Price adjustments
We do not offer price matching or price adjustments if an item goes on sale after you buy it.

## Order privacy
For your security, we only discuss an order with the person whose email address or phone number is on the order. If you contact us from a different email or number, we will ask you to confirm the email or phone number used at checkout.

## Contact hours
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Pacific time. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next business day.

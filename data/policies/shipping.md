# Shipping policy

## Where we ship
Pinecrest Supply ships to addresses in the United States and Canada only. We do not ship to other countries, to APO/FPO military addresses, or to freight forwarders.

## Processing time
Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 1 to 2 business days of being placed. Orders placed on weekends or US holidays start processing the next business day.

## Shipping speeds and costs
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days after the order ships and costs $6.95. Standard shipping is free on orders of $75 or more (before tax, after discounts). Expedited shipping takes 2 to 3 business days after the order ships and costs $14.95. Canadian orders take an extra 2 to 4 business days and may owe import duties to the carrier on delivery.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when the order ships. "Label created" means the carrier has not scanned the package yet; the first scan usually appears within 24 hours.

## Delays and delivery exceptions
Carrier delays and weather exceptions are outside our control, and the carrier's new estimate is the best information we have. If a package shows no tracking update for 5 business days, contact us and a teammate will open a trace with the carrier.

## Changing an address
An address can only be changed before the order ships. Reply to your order confirmation email with the new address. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed.

## Split shipments and backorders
If part of an order is backordered, we ship the available items first at no extra cost and send the rest when they arrive. Each shipment has its own tracking number.

## Cancelled orders
Cancelled orders are refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days of the cancellation.

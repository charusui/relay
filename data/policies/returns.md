# Returns and refunds policy

## Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Day 30 counts: an item delivered exactly 30 days ago can still be returned, and an item delivered 31 or more days ago cannot. The window starts on the delivery date shown in tracking.

## Condition
Returned items must be unused, unwashed and in their original condition with tags attached. Footwear must be tried on indoors only.

## Items that cannot be returned
Final-sale items cannot be returned: this includes everything sold as clearance and all socks (for hygiene reasons). Gift cards cannot be returned or refunded. Items that have not been delivered yet cannot be returned.

## How returns work
Returns are free. Once a return is opened, we email a prepaid return label. Pack the item, attach the label, and drop it with the carrier within 14 days.

## Refunds
Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse. Discounts used on the order are taken off the refund in proportion to the item's price. Original shipping charges are not refunded.

## Refund approval
Any return or refund worth more than $150 is reviewed by a member of our team before it is approved. The team replies on the same channel within one business day.

## Exchanges
We do not do direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the size or colour you want.
